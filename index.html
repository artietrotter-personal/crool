How to play

  1. The match. Stripes vs Solids, either over a set number of ends or racing to a points total. Pick the length on the match setup screen before you start. A tie goes to sudden death.
  2. The lag. Each team shoots toward the far rail. The ball that stops closest to the far rail takes the hammer for End 1. A pocketed lag ball loses.
  3. Each end. The cue starts on the foot spot, the 8-ball at center. Teams alternate seven shots each; the hammer shoots last.
  4. Shooting. Drag your ball anywhere fully behind the kitchen line. Drag above the kitchen to aim. Pull the power stick beside the table down to set power and let go to shoot; slide it back to the top before letting go to cancel.
  5. Kitchen line. After a shot, your team’s balls on or across the line are removed. Opponent balls go to the center spot, behind any ball already there.
  6. Cue contact. If your struck ball touches the cue, it is removed and your opponent places the cue anywhere. Knocking other balls into the cue is legal.
  7. Cue pocketed or on the line. The end stops. Opponent gets 2 points and the hammer. A match never ends this way; another end is played.
  8. The 8-ball. Pocketed or on the line, the shooting team places it anywhere.
  9. Chalk (can be turned off in Settings). Each shot wears the chalk off your tip; the chalk meter on the power stick shows what’s left. Tap the chalk cube on the rail to chalk up. Shoot with a bare tip and you risk a miscue.
  10. Scoring. The closest team scores a point for each ball nearer the cue than the opponent’s nearest. 8-ball closest or a tie: no points, hammer switches. Scoring team shoots first next end.
